<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Accumulative Balance">
    A mandatory system wallet where 20% of a user's marketing rewards are stored,
    usable only for NFT repurchases or upgrades. 120-day decay splits the unused
    balance differently by NFT type: **Regular NFT holders** — 70% to the DA
    Liquidity Pool, 30% to the upline sponsor; **Gift NFT holders** — 80% to
    the DA Liquidity Pool, 20% to the upline sponsor. In both cases, the sponsor
    share cascades up if the sponsor has zero Income Limit, and routes entirely
    to the Pool if no eligible upline exists.

    <Note>
      **120 days is a guaranteed minimum window, not an automatic expiry.** The
      `accumulativeDecayTime` check is enforced **only** inside
      `withdrawAccumulative()` — the admin/service-triggered redistribution path
      — and **not** on the spend or transfer paths. Within the first 120 days,
      the balance is guaranteed against redistribution. After day 120 the
      balance becomes **eligible** for redistribution, but the actual split is
      executed only when `withdrawAccumulative()` is invoked for that user by
      the SERVICE_ROLE or ADMIN_ROLE. Until that call lands, an "expired"
      Accumulative Balance is still spendable on NFT purchases/upgrades and
      transferable under the standard fees. As stated by the team in the audit
      response: *"Our goal is to guarantee the user's funds' integrity for a
      certain period of time, not to give them a lifecycle."*
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auto-sell">
    Automatic forced sale of DA if the user does not sell manually. The user receives
    70% of value in USDT (5% less than manual sale). 100% of auto-sold tokens are
    permanently burned; the remaining 30% stays as backing in the pool — this drives the price growth. DA is 100%
    available from day one — auto-sell triggers progressively from the remaining
    balance: Period 1 — 25% after 4 months, Period 2 — 40% of remaining after
    3 months, Period 3 — 50% of remaining after 3 months, Period 4 — 100% of
    remaining after 2 months. Total cycle: 12 months.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Autobuy (3/3)">
    Smart contract feature that automatically repurchases an NFT up to 3
    additional times after the original manual purchase when its Income Limit
    reaches zero, preventing lost marketing rewards. The 3/3 counter tracks
    only automatic renewals — the initial purchase is separate — so the same
    NFT level can be active a maximum of 4 times total (1 manual + 3 auto).
    Requires sufficient USDT on Regular or Accumulative Balance (both can be
    combined). Counter resets to 0/3 on NFT upgrade. Elite tier (L9–L10)
    supports unlimited autobuys.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Business Sale">
    Transfer of an entire business structure (partner tree) to another user
    through a DAO vote. The seller pays a fixed fee of 500 USDT (regulated
    by DAO) to execute the transfer; the buyer only receives the structure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CeFi (Centralized Finance)">
    Traditional financial services and products integrated into the ecosystem
    alongside DeFi components.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Compression">
    Smart contract algorithm that skips users whose Income Limit is zero or
    who lack the required NFT level, passing the reward upward to the next
    eligible user. Enables "infinite depth" earning.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cross-Chain Bridge">
    Managed cross-chain deposit service allowing users to fund their accounts
    from any supported blockchain. Deposits are processed on-chain via the
    SERVICE_ROLE through the DepositTR contract. This is a service-managed
    solution, not a trustless peer-to-peer bridge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DA (Deflationary Asset)">
    The ecosystem's core economic token. Hard-capped at 21M, 100% backed by
    a USDT liquidity pool. Price = Liquidity ÷ Supply. 100% of sold tokens are
    permanently burned on every sale. The remaining 25% (manual) / 30% (auto) stays
    as backing in the pool, directly driving price growth.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deflationary Cycle">
    A system-wide event triggered twice per year, lasting up to 30 days. During this
    period, NFTM mining, staking, and DA generation are temporarily paused. Automatic
    and voluntary DA sale mechanisms activate, with daily token burns reducing market
    supply and intensifying scarcity to strengthen the asset's value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DeFi (Decentralized Finance)">
    Financial services built on blockchain technology without intermediaries.
    One of the three pillars of the RWANFTFI ecosystem (RWA + DeFi + CeFi).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="FinPro (Financial Products)">
    A suite of financial products and services accessible through the platform.
    Launching Q3 2026.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Frozen Balance">
    When a marketing reward exceeds the user's current NFT Income Limit, the excess is
    placed into a Frozen Balance with a 72-hour countdown. The user must upgrade their NFT
    within 72 hours to unlock the funds — upon upgrading, frozen funds are released using
    the standard split (75% Regular Balance, 20% Accumulative Balance, 5% DA Liquidity Pool)
    and reduce the new NFT's Income Limit. If the user does not upgrade in time, frozen
    funds are forfeited — 70% goes to the DA Liquidity Pool for DA generation, 30% to the
    upline sponsor (cascades up if sponsor has zero Income Limit). If Autobuy is enabled, it fires first (refreshing the
    limit via rebuy); any remaining excess is still frozen with the same 72-hour timer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gift NFT">
    Non-sellable promotional NFT that places a user permanently in the binary tree.
    Cannot be purchased — awarded through promotions or Career Rank achievements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Income Limit">
    Maximum USDT rewards a specific NFT can generate before renewal is required.
    When limit reaches zero, all rewards are skipped (compression activates).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lending Default">
    If a borrower fails to repay their loan within the 30-day cutoff period, the
    collateralized DA enters the auto-sell cycle. Tokens are progressively burned
    through the standard 4-period schedule (25% → 40% → 50% → 100% of remaining
    balance over 12 months). The user keeps the original loan amount they received
    but loses the entire DA collateral; 100% of auto-sell proceeds are directed
    to the DA Liquidity Pool, not to the borrower. This mechanism protects system
    liquidity, reduces risk, and enforces financial discipline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lost Income">
    Notification that a user missed a marketing reward due to insufficient NFT level
    or exhausted income limit. Funds pass to the next qualified participant upline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Matching Bonus">
    Secondary reward system — 5% + 5% + 5% paid on income generated by direct
    partners across three levels. Unlocked at L4 NFT + personal sales milestones
    (1,000 and 3,000 USDT).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="NFTM">
    Internal mining counter (not a token) generated during the 40–45 day Mining phase.
    Must be staked within 72 hours to begin Farming DA. Has no market value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rebuy">
    Repurchasing an NFT of the same level to refresh the income limit.
    Available when remaining limit is 30% or less of initial value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="RWA (Real World Assets)">
    Physical assets (real estate, infrastructure, businesses) tokenized on blockchain.
    Represents the long-term strategic direction — launching 2027–2028.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Spillover">
    Automatic placement of an upline sponsor's invites into your binary tree. You earn
    Tree Distribution rewards from spillover partners based on their position in your
    structure (Level 2+), but you do NOT earn Sponsor Bonus — that goes to their actual
    inviter. The primary value is passive growth of your tree and rewards from deeper levels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="VERA AI">
    AI-powered personal financial assistant planned for 2027–2028.
    Designed to help users navigate the ecosystem's financial tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voucher (Gift Voucher)">
    User-generated digital coupon. The smart contract enforces a **minimum
    creation value of 5 USDT** — zero-value vouchers are rejected on-chain.
    Validity is **365 days** from issuance. Covers up to 100% of an NFT
    purchase. Transferable at 0% commission, except during the **Voucher
    Transfer Lock** — the last 24 hours before expiry, when transfers are
    blocked so a recipient cannot be handed a voucher they will not have time
    to use. **Cannot combine with Accumulative Balance** (valid combinations:
    Voucher + Regular, 100% Accumulative, or Accumulative + Regular). If a
    Voucher remains unused for its full 365-day lifetime, **100% of its value
    is directed to the DA Liquidity Pool**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
