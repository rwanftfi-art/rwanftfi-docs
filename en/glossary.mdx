* **Accumulative Balance:** A mandatory system wallet where 20% of a user's marketing rewards are stored, usable only for NFT repurchases or upgrades.
* **Auto-sell:** Automatic sale of DA when the TokenStack period expires. The user receives 70% (5% less than a manual sale).
* **Autobuy (3/3):** A feature allowing the smart contract to automatically repurchase an NFT up to three times when its Income Limit reaches zero, preventing lost marketing rewards.
* **Business Sale:** The transfer of an entire business structure (partner tree) to another user through a DAO vote. Commission: 500 USDT (regulated by DAO).
* **CeFi (Centralized Finance):** Traditional financial services and products integrated into the ecosystem alongside DeFi components.
* **Compression:** A smart contract algorithm that skips users whose Income Limit is zero or who do not have the required NFT level to earn from a specific depth, passing the reward upward to the next eligible user.
* **DA (Deflationary Asset):** The ecosystem's core economic token. Hard-capped at 21M, 100% backed by a USDT liquidity pool, and mathematically designed to increase in price as tokens are burned upon sale.
* **deBridge:** A decentralized cross-chain bridge integrated into the platform for funding accounts from other blockchains.
* **DeFi (Decentralized Finance):** Financial services built on blockchain technology without intermediaries, forming one of the three pillars of the RWANFTFI ecosystem.
* **FinPro (Financial Products):** A suite of financial products and services accessible through the platform, launching in Q3 2026.
* **Gift NFT:** A non-sellable promotional NFT that places a user permanently in the binary tree for team building, awarded through promotions or Career Rank achievements.
* **Income Limit:** The maximum amount of USDT rewards a specific NFT can generate before it must be renewed or upgraded.
* **Lost Income:** A notification that a user missed a marketing reward due to an insufficient NFT level or an exhausted income limit. The funds bypass the user and move to the next qualified participant higher up in the structure.
* **Matching Bonus:** A secondary reward system (5% + 5% + 5%) paid on the income generated by a user's direct partners across three levels, unlocked by achieving specific personal sales thresholds.
* **NFTM:** An intermediate metric (or counter) generated during the 40-45 day Mining phase, which must be staked within 72 hours to begin Farming DA.
* **Rebuy:** Repurchasing an NFT of the same level to refresh the income limit. Available when the remaining limit is ≤ 30% of the initial value.
* **RWA (Real World Assets):** Physical assets (real estate, infrastructure, businesses) tokenized on the blockchain, representing the long-term strategic direction of the RWANFTFI ecosystem.
* **Spillover:** The automatic placement of new users into a participant's binary tree by upline sponsors, generating passive turnover and rewards.
* **VERA AI:** An AI-powered personal financial assistant planned for integration in 2027-2028, designed to help users navigate the ecosystem's financial tools.
* **Voucher (Gift Voucher):** A user-generated digital coupon (minimum 5 USDT, 365-day validity) that can cover up to 100% of an NFT purchase, transferable at 0% commission.
