RWANFTFI is building a global financial ecosystem bridging RWA, DeFi, and CeFi. We actively seek partnerships across multiple categories.

## Current Integrations

The protocol already runs on production-grade Web3 infrastructure with the following integrations live at launch:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Binance Smart Chain (BSC)" icon="link" href="https://www.bnbchain.org/en/bnb-smart-chain">
    The protocol runs exclusively on BNB Smart Chain using the BEP20 standard. All contracts, balances, and governance wallets are publicly verifiable on [BscScan](https://bscscan.com/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="CertiK — Independent Audit" icon="shield-check" href="https://skynet.certik.com/projects/rwanftfi">
    Smart contracts independently audited by CertiK. Final report published April 28, 2026, with verification on the public CertiK Skynet directory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="USDT (Tether) — BEP20" icon="coins" href="https://tether.to/">
    USDT is the unit of account, payment medium, and 100% backing of the DA token. All financial operations clear in USDT on BEP20.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MetaMask, Trust Wallet & EVM Wallets" icon="wallet">
    Any EVM-compatible Web3 wallet can sign transactions — including [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/) and [Trust Wallet](https://trustwallet.com/). Email-and-password sign-in gives view-only access; financial actions require a wallet signature.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
