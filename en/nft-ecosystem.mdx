## Protocol Reward Caps & Auto-Renewal
<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How Protocol Reward Caps Work" icon="gauge-high">
    Every NFT has a **Protocol Reward Cap** — the on-chain term is *Income Limit*. The cap is consumed by eligible partner rewards and DA-sale payouts. When it reaches zero, affected reward and DA-sale functions pause until the NFT is renewed or upgraded under the current protocol rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Autobuy (3/3 System)" icon="rotate">
    Autobuy automatically renews an eligible NFT when its Protocol Reward Cap reaches zero. Levels 1–8 currently allow up to three automatic renewals after the original purchase. Elite tiers use unlimited Autobuy under the current configuration. Every Autobuy requires sufficient eligible USDT balance. Unused Protocol Reward Cap from the previous NFT does not carry over to a replacement or upgraded NFT.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Partner Structure Transfer" icon="handshake">
    A DAO-authorized process can transfer the complete partner structure to another user. The seller pays the current on-chain transfer fee. The buyer receives the structure after execution. Individual NFT sales without the associated structure are not supported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
<Note>
  **The Protocol Reward Cap is a technical limit.** It is not expected earnings and does not guarantee that the full amount will be credited.
</Note>

### Frozen Balance — Temporary Buffer on Qualifying Direct Activity

When a qualifying Sponsor Bonus from a direct invitee's first NFT purchase or Autobuy exceeds the holder's remaining Protocol Reward Cap, the excess is held for 72 hours under the Frozen Balance rules.

## Gift NFTs & Vouchers
<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Gift NFTs" icon="gift">
    Gift NFTs are non-sellable promotional assets that can place users in the partner network. They cannot be purchased and are awarded under approved promotions or rank programs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gift Vouchers" icon="ticket">
    Gift Vouchers are user-created coupons subject to the documented minimum value, lifetime, transfer restriction, and payment-combination rules. If unused for the full lifetime, the value is routed according to the current smart-contract rules.
