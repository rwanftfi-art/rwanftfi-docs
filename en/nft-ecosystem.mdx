Owning an active NFT is required to access the partner program, DA mining, and the financial tools attached to the selected tier. One active NFT is supported per account; an upgrade replaces the previous NFT. Every purchased NFT, from Genesis to Infinity, also opens FinPro: its holder can open a FinFlow position from 25 USDT.

## Protocol Reward Caps & Auto-Renewal
<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How Protocol Reward Caps Work" icon="gauge-high">
    Every NFT has a **Protocol Reward Cap** — the on-chain term is *Income Limit*. The cap is consumed by eligible partner rewards and DA-sale payouts. When it reaches zero, affected reward and DA-sale functions pause until the NFT is renewed or upgraded under the current protocol rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Autobuy (3/3 System)" icon="rotate">
    Autobuy automatically renews an eligible NFT when its Protocol Reward Cap reaches zero. Levels 1–8 currently allow up to three automatic renewals after the original purchase. Elite tiers use unlimited Autobuy under the current configuration. Every Autobuy requires sufficient eligible USDT balance. Unused Protocol Reward Cap from the previous NFT does not carry over to a replacement or upgraded NFT.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Business Sale (Partner Structure Transfer)" icon="handshake">
    Business Sale, the DAO-authorized transfer of a complete partner structure to another user, is currently **disabled** by DAO vote following an account-security incident. It can be re-enabled only by a new DAO vote. When the function is enabled, the seller pays the current on-chain transfer fee and the buyer receives the structure after execution. Individual NFT sales without the associated structure are not supported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
<Note>
  **The Protocol Reward Cap is a technical limit.** It is not expected earnings and does not guarantee that the full amount will be credited.
</Note>

### Frozen Balance — Temporary Buffer on Qualifying Direct Activity

When a qualifying Sponsor Bonus from a direct invitee's first NFT purchase or Autobuy exceeds the holder's remaining Protocol Reward Cap, the excess is held for 72 hours under the Frozen Balance rules.

## Gift NFTs & Vouchers

### Gift NFTs

Gift NFTs cannot be bought or sold. They are awarded under promotions such as First Purchase NFT and Upgrade NFT, under the Career Ranks and the Fast Start Program, and to active leaders. A holder can pass a Gift NFT on to a future partner.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A place in the network" icon="sitemap">
    A Gift NFT takes a permanent place in the partner network and carries its own Protocol Reward Cap.
  </Card>
  <Card title="50 / 50 split" icon="scale-balanced">
    Rewards on a Gift NFT are split equally between the Regular Balance and the Accumulative Balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="FinPro access" icon="chart-line">
    A Gift NFT opens FinFlow and can hold a position of its own within its limit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Up to 100 per address" icon="layer-group">
    The current on-chain limit on Gift NFTs held by one address.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Current promotions are listed in [Rewards & Compensation](/en/marketing-rewards#active-promotions).

### Gift Vouchers

Gift Vouchers are user-created coupons subject to the documented minimum value, lifetime, transfer restriction, and payment-combination rules. If unused for the full lifetime, the value is routed according to the current smart-contract rules.
