### Frozen Balance — 72-Hour Upgrade Window

When a marketing reward is generated that exceeds your NFT's remaining Income Limit, the smart contract does not discard the excess. Instead, it splits the reward into two parts:

- **Within your remaining limit** — credited normally using the standard split: **75% Regular Balance**, **20% Accumulative Balance**, **5% DA Liquidity Pool**.
- **Excess above your limit** — placed into a **Frozen Balance** with a **72-hour countdown timer**.

You then have exactly **72 hours** to upgrade your NFT to a higher level (which carries a higher Income Limit). Upon upgrading, the frozen funds are released and credited using the same 75/20/5 split. The released amount is deducted from your **new** NFT's Income Limit.

<Warning>
  **72-Hour Deadline:** If you do not upgrade within 72 hours, the frozen funds are **permanently forfeited** and redistributed: **70%** is directed to the DA Liquidity Pool for DA generation, and **30%** is transferred to your upline sponsor in the network.

  **Cascade rule (identical to the Accumulative Balance 120-day decay cascade):** If the direct upline sponsor's Income Limit is exhausted (zero), the 30% sponsor share passes further up the chain to the next participant with an active Income Limit. If **no one** in the entire upline has an active Income Limit, the 30% sponsor share is also routed to the DA Liquidity Pool — so **100% of the forfeited amount ends up in the DA Liquidity Pool**. Either way, you receive nothing — the opportunity is lost.
</Warning>

**Interaction with Autobuy:**
If Autobuy is enabled, the smart contract evaluates the rebuy **before** any portion of the reward is moved into Frozen Balance. The sequence is strictly ordered:

1. The same-level NFT is automatically repurchased (subject to the standard 3/3 cap on L1–L8 — see Autobuy above), refreshing your Income Limit. Funds for the rebuy are drawn from Regular and/or Accumulative Balance.
2. The pending reward is re-evaluated against the refreshed Income Limit; as much as fits is credited normally with the 75/20/5 split.
3. Only the residual that still does not fit after step 2 is placed into Frozen Balance with the 72-hour timer.

You can then manually upgrade to a higher-level NFT within the 72-hour window to unlock the rest.

<Info>
  **Example:** You hold a small NFT with an Income Limit of 160 USDT. Your direct partner purchases an NFT for 2,200 USDT, generating a 30% Direct Sale commission of 660 USDT.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Commission is calculated">
    Your partner buys a 2,200 USDT NFT → 30% Direct Sale commission = **660 USDT** earned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount within your Income Limit is credited">
    160 USDT fits within your current limit and is split: 75% Regular Balance, 20% Accumulative Balance, 5% DA Liquidity Pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Excess is frozen for 72 hours">
    The remaining **500 USDT** exceeds your Income Limit and is placed into Frozen Balance. A 72-hour countdown begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You upgrade your NFT">
    You upgrade to Eclipse (Income Limit: 650 USDT). The 500 USDT frozen balance is released and credited using the standard 75/20/5 split. Your new remaining limit: 650 − 500 = **150 USDT**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Bulk processing of expired freezes (technical):** When the protocol resolves expired Frozen Balances at scale, the smart contract walks the queue in **paginated batches with a hard cap on the number of entries handled per call**. This is a deliberate gas-exhaustion safeguard — it prevents a single transaction from being pushed into a denial-of-service condition by an unbounded freeze queue. For an individual user this is invisible during normal operation; in the rare case of an unusually large queue, full resolution may simply take more than one transaction to complete. The economic outcome for your funds — release on upgrade within 72 hours, or the 70/30 redistribution described above after the deadline — is unchanged.
</Note>
