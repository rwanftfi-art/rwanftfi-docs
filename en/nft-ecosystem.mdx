The central product of the RWANFTFI ecosystem is the NFT itself. Owning an NFT is mandatory to participate in the structural marketing, mine Deflationary Assets (DA), and access the suite of financial tools. Users can only hold one original active NFT per account at a time. When purchasing a higher-level NFT, the old one is automatically replaced by the new one.

## Tier Overview

Regular NFTs are the primary entry point into the ecosystem. They are divided into three tiers — **Basic** (Levels 1–4), **Premium** (Levels 5–8), and **Elite** (Levels 9–10). The level of the NFT dictates the maximum income limit, the depth of marketing levels the user can earn from, and the specific financial features unlocked. The platform features exactly 10 NFT levels priced in USDT.

## Income Limits & Auto-Renewal

Every NFT has a strict Income Limit. Once a user earns marketing rewards equal to this limit, the limit reaches zero, and the user misses all further rewards. To prevent this, users must renew their NFT. Users cannot manually repurchase the exact same NFT if their remaining limit is above 30%.

**Autobuy (3/3 System):** For all NFTs except the top two Elite levels, users can activate an "Autobuy" function. If activated, the system automatically repurchases the same level NFT up to 3 times to prevent missed rewards, provided there are sufficient USDT funds on the available or accumulative balances. After 3 uses, the function is locked for that specific NFT, but resets back to 3/3 if the user upgrades to a higher level.

<Warning>
  **Income Limit:** Once your NFT reaches its income limit, you stop receiving ALL marketing rewards. Enable Autobuy to prevent this — or manually renew when your limit drops below 30%.
</Warning>

**Business Sale:**
Users can sell their entire business structure (partner tree and downline) to another user through a DAO-authorized process. A fixed commission of $500 is charged per business sale. This commission amount can be adjusted through DAO voting. Note: selling an individual NFT as a standalone asset (without the structure) is not supported — the NFT is tied to the account and business structure.

## Gift NFTs & Vouchers

To facilitate network growth and promotions, the system includes special promotional assets.

<Info>
  **Gift NFTs** are non-sellable promotional assets that place users permanently in the binary tree for team building. They cannot be purchased — they are awarded through promotions or Career Rank achievements.
</Info>

**Gift Vouchers:** Users can generate Gift Vouchers of any denomination starting from 5 USDT, with a validity period of 365 days. Vouchers can be transferred to other users with a 0% commission. They can be used to cover up to 100% of an NFT purchase price. Vouchers can be combined with the Regular Balance, but cannot be combined with the Accumulative Balance. Vouchers cannot be transferred on the very last day of their 365-day lifetime.

## Ambassador & VIP Edition

These are highly exclusive, limited-edition NFTs reserved for top performers and strategic partners.

* **Ambassador NFT:** Limited to 100 units. These NFTs have no income limits and do not participate in standard farming. They have their own dedicated reward system. They are not transferred if a user executes a Business Sale.
* **VIP Edition NFT:** Limited to 100 units. Access is granted as a reward for achieving the "Leader" Career Rank or higher.
