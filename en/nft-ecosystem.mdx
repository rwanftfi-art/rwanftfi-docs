The central product of the RWANFTFI ecosystem is the NFT itself. Owning an NFT is mandatory to participate in the structural marketing, mine Deflationary Assets (DA), and access the suite of financial tools. Users can only hold one original active NFT per account at a time. When purchasing a higher-level NFT, the old one is automatically replaced by the new one.

## Tier Overview

Regular NFTs are the primary entry point into the ecosystem. They are divided into three tiers — **Basic** (Levels 1–4), **Premium** (Levels 5–8), and **Elite** (Levels 9–10). The level of the NFT dictates the maximum income limit, the depth of marketing levels the user can earn from, and the specific financial features unlocked. The platform features exactly 10 NFT levels priced in USDT.

## Income Limits & Auto-Renewal
<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How Income Limits Work" icon="gauge-high">
    Every NFT has a strict Income Limit. Once you earn marketing rewards
    equal to this limit, it reaches zero and you **miss all further rewards**.
    To prevent this, renew your NFT. You cannot manually repurchase if your
    remaining limit is above 30%.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Autobuy (3/3 System)" icon="rotate">
    For all NFTs except Elite (L9–L10), activate Autobuy to automatically
    repurchase the same NFT up to 3 times when limit hits zero. Requires
    sufficient USDT on available or accumulative balance. Resets to 3/3
    on NFT upgrade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Business Sale" icon="handshake">
    Sell your entire business structure (partner tree and downline) to another
    user through a DAO-authorized process. Fixed commission: 500 USDT
    (adjustable via DAO). Individual NFT sales without the structure
    are not supported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
<Warning>
  **Income Limit:** Once your NFT reaches its income limit, you stop receiving
  ALL marketing rewards. Enable Autobuy to prevent this — or manually renew
  when your limit drops below 30%.
</Warning>
## Gift NFTs & Vouchers
<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Gift NFTs" icon="gift">
    Non-sellable promotional assets that place users permanently in the binary
    tree. Cannot be purchased — awarded through promotions or Career Rank
    achievements. Unlimited per account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gift Vouchers" icon="ticket">
    User-generated coupons from 5 USDT, valid 365 days. Cover up to 100%
    of NFT purchase. Transferable at 0% commission. Cannot combine
    with Accumulative Balance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
<Warning>
  **Gift NFT Overwrite:** If you already own a Regular NFT and activate
  a Gift NFT, the Gift NFT will **overwrite** your Regular NFT. Only activate
  Gift NFTs on accounts without a Regular NFT.
</Warning>
