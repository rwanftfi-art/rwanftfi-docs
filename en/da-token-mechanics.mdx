DA is a non-transferable BEP-20 protocol utility token with a maximum supply of 21,000,000 units. Its internal protocol price is calculated using the designated USDT liquidity-pool balance and circulating DA supply.

  </Card>
  <Card title="USDT Liquidity Pool" icon="droplet">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Burn on Eligible Sales" icon="fire">

  </Card>
  <Card title="No P2P Transfers" icon="ban">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How does the protocol price formula work?

DA protocol price = designated USDT liquidity-pool balance ÷ circulating DA supply.

The price changes when either liquidity or circulating supply changes. The formula does not independently guarantee liquidity, redemption availability, or a specific user outcome.

<Info>
  **Seed Supply** is a small permanent reserve used as a technical safeguard for the pricing function. It is excluded from user circulation, sales, farming, lending, and partner distributions.
</Info>

## Instant Access & Auto-Sell Cycle

### Instant Access

DA credited after Farming is available under the current protocol rules. Eligible users may sell manually, use a supported batch as lending collateral, or hold the DA until the scheduled Auto-Sell events.

### Auto-Sell Cycle

| Period | Timing | Share of Remaining DA |
|:---:|---|:---:|
| 1 | After 120 days | 25% |
| 2 | After another 90 days | 40% |
| 3 | After another 90 days | 50% |
| 4 | After another 65 days | 100% |

Each Auto-Sell uses the DA protocol price applicable at execution. Future protocol prices are not predetermined.

## How does multi-batch lending against DA work?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete a Mining and Farming cycle">
    A completed cycle creates an eligible DA batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use that batch as collateral">
    The batch serves as independent collateral under the current lending rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete another cycle">
    A new cycle creates a separate batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a separate loan">
    A separate eligible loan may be opened against the new batch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each loan is tied to one DA batch. Default processing affects only the pledged batch, not all DA held by the user.

For details, see [Selling & Lending](/en/da-selling-lending) and [Lending Against DA](/en/da-lending).

## What is the biannual Deflationary Cycle?

<Steps>
  <Step title="New cycles may be paused">
    New Mining, staking, and DA-generation cycles may be paused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sale mechanisms continue">
    Eligible sale mechanisms continue under the active configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DA is processed and burned">
    DA is processed and burned under the documented rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Existing cycles continue">
    Existing cycles started before the event continue on schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The Deflationary Cycle is a protocol event. Current dates, duration, and active parameters are published before each cycle.
</Info>
