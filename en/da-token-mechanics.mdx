The core economic engine of the RWANFTFI ecosystem is the Deflationary Asset (DA). This token model is engineered to be 100% backed by USDT, with deflationary mechanics where tokens are burned upon sale, mathematically reducing supply relative to the liquidity pool.

## Token Parameters

The DA token operates on the Binance Smart Chain (BEP20) as an upgradeable ERC20 token with 6 decimals.

**Core Parameters:**
* **Maximum Supply:** Hard-capped at 21,000,000 (twenty-one million) tokens.
* **Initial Price:** 1 USDT per DA.
* **Transferability:** Peer-to-peer transfers of DA tokens between users are strictly forbidden at the smart contract level. The token can only be interacted with via official ecosystem functions (sell, loan, repay).
* **Liquidity Pool:** Every income source in the ecosystem feeds into the DA liquidity pool. A 5% commission-tax on all marketing rewards is directly routed to this pool. This 5% commission is deducted from marketing rewards at the time of accrual. It is separate from and unrelated to the sell percentages (75% manual / 70% auto-sell) described in [Selling & Lending](/en/da-selling-lending).

## The "Only Upward" Price Formula

The price of DA is determined dynamically by the smart contract based on the ratio of the USDT liquidity pool to the total circulating supply of DA.

`Price = Liquidity ÷ Circulating Supply`

*(The smart contract internally uses 6-decimal precision: `Price = (Liquidity × 10^6) / TotalSupply`, but the economic principle is straightforward — price equals liquidity divided by supply.)*

Because tokens are burned upon sale (reducing supply) while fees remain in the pool (maintaining or increasing liquidity), the mathematical output of this formula constantly drives the price upward.

<Tip>
  **Why does the price only go up?** Every time DA is sold, tokens are burned (removed from supply), but the USDT stays in the liquidity pool. Less tokens ÷ same or more USDT = higher price per token.
</Tip>
