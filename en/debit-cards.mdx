Cryptocurrency in the wallet becomes fiat on the card: the card is topped up with stablecoins and pays everywhere Visa is accepted, online and offline, including Apple Pay and Google Pay. The card currency is US dollars. Cards are issued by a licensed banking partner under a Visa license.

<Note>
  **Sales are open.** Card sales started on 29 September 2026; the first wave is 5,000 cards. Cards are bought on the official platform, [rwanftcards.com](https://rwanftcards.com).
</Note>

- After three years the Plan is renewed at its then-current price and a new card is issued; without renewal the card is deactivated.
- A Plan can be upgraded at any time: the same card and card number with the new Plan's limits.
- Verification is passed once, and an account can hold several cards, each with its own balance and limits.
- Cards are personal: only the verified holder may use them, and passing a card to anyone else, relatives included, is prohibited.

## What you pay

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Plan price | Paid once for the three-year term |
| Card maintenance | \$0 for the whole Plan term |
| Top-up fee | 3.5% on Standard, 2% on Premium, Black and Signature |
| Card transaction | A fixed \$0.30 per transaction |
| Cross-border fee | About 0.5% when the merchant is outside the card's issuing jurisdiction or the payment is not in USD |
| Inactivity | After 30 days without activity the card is frozen for security and unfrozen via support; after 12 months of inactivity a fee of up to \$5 per month may apply |
| Reissue | Virtual card \$30, plastic card \$150, metal card \$300 |
