The AML Checker is part of the RWANFTFI application: open the **AML** tab in the sidebar or go to [app.rwanftfi.com/aml-checker](https://app.rwanftfi.com/aml-checker). Checking a counterparty address before sending or receiving funds helps avoid interacting with addresses linked to sanctions, mixers, scams or hacks.

## How to run a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Connect your wallet in the application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the network and check type">
    Select the blockchain and the type of check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address">
    Paste the wallet address you want to check and press **Check**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a package and pay">
    Pick a package and confirm the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>
