## Security Guidance

<Warning>
  - Never share a seed phrase, private key, financial password, TAN code, or two-factor authentication code.
  - Share your **Secret Code** only with official support, and only in a security-related request. Support cannot process a security request without it.
  - Support never asks for a seed phrase, a private key, or a prepayment to "unlock" funds or an account. Any such request is a scam.
  - Verify the domain against the [list of official domains](/en/security#official-domains) and check contract addresses.
  - Report impersonation and phishing through the official security contact.
</Warning>
