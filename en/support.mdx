## Platform Security

<Warning>
  **Watch out for scams and impersonators!** RWANFTFI team members will never DM you first or ask for your private keys, seed phrases, or funds. Always verify you are on the official website: **rwanftfi.com**
</Warning>

## Telegram Bot

The platform includes an integrated Telegram Bot for security and notifications:
- Authorization and account linking
- Transaction verification codes
- Lost Income notifications
- Security alerts

<Info>
  The Telegram Bot must be linked to exactly one account. It is required for receiving security codes and platform notifications.
</Info>

## Web3 Wallet Requirements

<Note>
  While you can log into the web dashboard with email/password to view data, executing any financial transaction (withdrawal, purchase, transfer) **requires** a connected Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.).
</Note>
