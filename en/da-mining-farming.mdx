## How do you acquire DA through Mining and Farming?

Only eligible Premium and Elite NFT tiers can access this process. An active NFT with a non-zero Protocol Reward Cap is required to start a new cycle.

<Info>
  **NFTM is an internal mining counter.** It is not a transferable token, has no independent market value, and exists only within the documented Mining and Farming process.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate Mining">
    Start a cycle on an eligible NFT. Duration ranges from 45 days for Hydro to 40 days for Infinity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate NFTM">
    Levels 5–7 currently generate one 10% NFTM cycle; Levels 8–10 generate a 10% first cycle and a 15% second cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit Within 72 Hours">
    Completed NFTM must be committed to Farming within the claim window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Farm DA">
    After Farming completes, the protocol calculates the eligible DA amount using the current formula and credits it to the user's TokenStack.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **72-Hour Claim Window:** If completed NFTM is not committed within 72 hours, the cycle resets under the smart-contract rules.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Claim Before You Upgrade:** An NFT purchase or upgrade invokes `terminate()` on the current Mining and Farming state. Claim any available matured result before replacing or upgrading the NFT.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Deflationary Cycle Pause:** During an active Deflationary Cycle, launching new cycles may be paused. Existing cycles continue and complete on schedule.
</Warning>
