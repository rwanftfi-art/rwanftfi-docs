Brand protection is a foundational layer of the RWANFTFI ecosystem. The protocol's name and identity are formally registered with the **[UK Intellectual Property Office (UKIPO)](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/intellectual-property-office)** — one of the most respected and globally recognised trademark authorities. The application is publicly searchable on the official UKIPO registry, creating a legally enforceable layer of brand ownership on top of the corporate and technology framework.

## Verify in 60 seconds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the UKIPO registry" icon="globe">
    Go to the official UK Intellectual Property Office trademark search at [trademarks.ipo.gov.uk](https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmcase/page/Results/1/UK00004369823). No login or signup required — it is a free public registry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the case file" icon="file-lines">
    The page that loads is the live, official record. Cross-check the **Trademark (RWANFT)**, **Application Number (UK00004369823)**, **Owner (RWANFTFI Corporation)**, **Filing Date (07 April 2026)** and **Class 42** with the details listed above — they will match exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the representative" icon="briefcase">
    The case file lists **Gresham International** (London) as the appointed IPO representative — a regulated UK firm specialising in trademark prosecution.
  </Step>
</Steps>
