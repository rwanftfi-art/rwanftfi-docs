RWANFTFI is a Web3 protocol that expands the utility of Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) by turning them into membership-based access keys for a structured ecosystem. The protocol, built on BNB Smart Chain, combines NFT access tiers, DA mechanics, partner-program functionality and governance with live ecosystem services: the FinPro financial products, the RWA NFT Cards Visa debit card, a cross-chain bridge and an AML Checker. Mining, farming and lending are scheduled for activation in 2027, and verified Real World Asset (RWA) integrations remain on the roadmap.

## How does the Deflationary Asset (DA) power the ecosystem?

The ecosystem uses **DA**, a non-transferable BEP-20 protocol utility token with a maximum supply of 21,000,000 units. Its internal protocol price is calculated from the designated [USDT](https://tether.to/) liquidity-pool balance and the circulating DA supply. DA issuance, sales, burns, lending, and liquidity depend on the current smart-contract parameters.

RWANFTFI operates on [BNB Smart Chain](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/bnb-smart-chain) for fast, low-cost transactions. The [cross-chain bridge](/en/bridge), a managed service operated through the documented `SERVICE_ROLE`, processes deposits from 14 supported networks.

## How do you participate in RWANFTFI?

Participants acquire one of **10 NFT access tiers**. Each tier defines the available protocol features, Partner Network Depth, Protocol Reward Cap, mining and farming eligibility, lending access, and renewal conditions. Users can participate in eligible partner-program activity, access DA mechanics, and use the financial tools available for their tier under the current protocol rules. Every purchased NFT also opens FinPro, the ecosystem's line of financial products.

## How is DA designed to support long-term protocol activity?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multiple Liquidity Sources" icon="droplet">
    The DA Liquidity Pool receives funds from active protocol allocations and fees. Since the Phase 2 activation in June 2026, the Token Reserve share of NFT sales is 15% of every first purchase and 18% of every rebuy. External sources such as RWA income, payment-service commissions, swap commissions, and FinPro revenue are shown as active only once their flow into the pool is publicly documented, even where the service itself is already live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Three Burn Mechanisms" icon="fire">
    - DA processed through eligible manual sales is burned.
    - DA processed through Auto-Sell is burned.
    - Collateral processed after lending default is burned progressively under the applicable schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Biannual Deflationary Cycle" icon="rotate">
    The protocol documentation describes a scheduled Deflationary Cycle that may pause new mining, staking, and DA-generation cycles for up to 30 days while existing cycles continue. The first cycle has not been held yet; its date and parameters are published on the official channels before the event. Explore the full mechanics in [DA Token Mechanics](/en/da-token-mechanics).
  </Accordion>
