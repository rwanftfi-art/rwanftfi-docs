## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is RWANFTFI?">
    RWANFTFI is a Web3 protocol on BNB Smart Chain where NFT access tiers unlock selected ecosystem features: the partner program, the FinPro financial products, DA mechanics, and governance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What blockchain does RWANFTFI run on?">
    RWANFTFI operates on BNB Smart Chain using BEP-20 compatible contracts. BNB is required for gas fees.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which domains are official?">
    rwanftfi.com and its subdomains app, news, bridge, brandbook, whitepaper, and share; rwafinpro.com for FinPro; and rwanftcards.com for the debit cards. Any other domain is not official. See [Official Domains](/en/security#official-domains).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Mining, Farming, and Lending available now?">
    No. NFTM Mining, DA Farming, and DA Lending are documented mechanics scheduled for activation in 2027. Activation dates will be published on the official channels.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## NFTs and the Partner Program

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many NFT tiers exist and what do they cost?">
    There are 10 documented tiers from Genesis at 28 USDT to Infinity at 24,000 USDT. See the [NFT Ecosystem page](/en/nft-ecosystem).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the Protocol Reward Cap?">
    The maximum cumulative eligible rewards credited during the current NFT cycle. On-chain term: *Income Limit*.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the Accumulative Balance, and why is its lifetime 180 days?">
    A restricted internal balance used under documented purchase, transfer, fee, and redistribution rules. In September 2026 a DAO vote extended its lifetime from 120 to 180 days while a timer-reset bug is fixed; a separate vote is planned to return it to 120 days after the fix.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Frozen Balance?">
    A 72-hour buffer for a qualifying direct Sponsor Bonus above the remaining Protocol Reward Cap. See the [Frozen Balance page](/en/frozen-balance).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does the partner network work?">
    The contract assigns permanent positions; eligibility depends on tier, depth, activity, phase, and cap.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What partner-network depth does each NFT tier support?">
    The current values are listed in the [NFT tier table](/en/nft-ecosystem#all-10-tiers-at-a-glance); future levels require governance activation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the referral link format?">
    `share.rwanftfi.com/?ref=CODE`. Your referral code is permanent; the old link format stopped working in August 2026.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is a Gift NFT?">
    An NFT that cannot be bought or sold; it is awarded under promotions and programs. It takes a permanent place in the partner network with its own Protocol Reward Cap, its rewards are split 50/50 between the Regular and Accumulative balances, and it opens FinFlow. See [Gift NFTs](/en/nft-ecosystem#gift-nfts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happened to Business Sale?">
    Business Sale, the transfer of a complete partner structure, has been disabled by DAO vote since June 2026, after an account-security incident. Only a new DAO vote can re-enable it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FinPro

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are FinPro and FinFlow?">
    FinPro is the ecosystem's line of financial products at rwafinpro.com. FinFlow, the first product, has been live since 8 July 2026: a position in a market-neutral strategy, represented by a non-transferable FinFlow NFT and opened from 25 USDT. Accruals are not guaranteed. See [FinPro & FinFlow](/en/finpro-finflow).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is the FinFlow limit calculated?">
    The base FinFlow limit of an NFT equals its Protocol Reward Cap. The limits of NFTs bought tier by tier are summed; a higher tier bought directly brings only its own limit. While FinFlow Promotion 2026 runs, the promotional limits apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use FinFlow with a Gift NFT?">
    Yes. A Gift NFT opens FinFlow and can hold a position of its own within its limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is FinPro Marketing?">
    A separate partner program for FinPro products, with its own structure: Direct Sale plus 20 levels, credited 100% to the marketing balance without the 75/20/5 split. It starts with the Claim Spot pre-launch; marketing accruals begin with the FinCore launch. See [FinPro Marketing](/en/finpro-marketing).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ecosystem Services

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get the RWA NFT Cards debit card?">
    On rwanftcards.com: register, pass verification, buy a Plan, and activate the card with a first top-up of $20 or more. See [Debit Cards](/en/debit-cards).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does the bridge work?">
    The bridge at bridge.rwanftfi.com processes deposits into the ecosystem from 14 networks, so assets do not have to be moved to BNB Smart Chain first. See [Cross-Chain Bridge](/en/bridge).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the AML Checker?">
    A paid check of a wallet address before a transaction: a risk score across 19 blockchains and 14 stablecoins, with links to sanctions, mixers, scams, and hacks. See [AML Checker](/en/aml-checker).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## DA

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the DA protocol price calculated?">
    The designated USDT liquidity-pool balance is divided by circulating DA supply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can DA be transferred between users?">
    No. DA is non-transferable and can be used only through supported protocol functions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the Auto-Sell cycle?">
    Unprocessed DA is scheduled across four periods over 365 days under the current parameters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is NFTM?">
    An internal Mining counter used in Farming; not a transferable token.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I take a loan against DA?">
    Eligible L5–L10 holders will be able to use a supported DA batch as collateral under the documented rules once DA Lending is activated in 2027. See [Lending Against DA](/en/da-lending).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the protocol audited?">
    CertiK reviewed the contracts and code version within the published audit scope. See [Security & Audit](/en/security).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the Secret Code?">
    A random word generated together with the financial password. Support requires it for any security-related request. Share it only with official support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I keep my account safe?">
    Use official domains, protect wallet credentials, and verify every transaction before signing. Support never writes to you first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my account is hacked?">
    Contact official support; security-related requests require your Secret Code. The team has no access to users' businesses: only a DAO vote can move a business to a new wallet, as it did for a hacked account in June 2026.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

    EIP-2535 Diamond architecture with eight functional facets and role-based permissions. See [Architecture](/en/architecture).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who governs the protocol?">
    GovToken holders under the documented current allocation and thresholds. See [Governance — DAO](/en/governance).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How are RWANFTFI NFTs described in the platform terms?">
    As digital access products providing eligibility for specified ecosystem features; no guaranteed income, fixed yield, or resale value. See [Legal & Compliance](/en/legal).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
