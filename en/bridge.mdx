The RWANFTFI ecosystem runs on BNB Smart Chain. The bridge is the managed cross-chain deposit service that lets users fund the ecosystem from other networks without moving assets to BNB Smart Chain on their own first.

## Supported networks

**14 networks:** BNB Chain, Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Solana, Bitcoin, Optimism, Celo, Sonic, XRPL, Linea, Hyperliquid and HyperEVM.

## Bridge and debit cards

Top-up of [debit cards](/en/debit-cards) with any popular cryptocurrency through the built-in bridge, auto-converted to dollars, is planned for Q4 2026.

<Warning>
  Use only **bridge.rwanftfi.com**. Check the domain before connecting a wallet, and never share a seed phrase or private key: the bridge never asks for them.
</Warning>
