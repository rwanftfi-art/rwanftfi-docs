When a user activates an NFT (either Regular or Gift), they are permanently placed into a binary marketing structure. The structure has a maximum depth of 22 levels. When fully filled to Level 22, the tree holds exactly **8,388,606 participant positions**, growing exponentially at each level (2 on Level 1, 4 on Level 2, 8 on Level 3, and so on).

The smart contract automatically places new users using a "weakest branch, left to right" algorithm. A new participant is permanently linked to their sponsor. If a sponsor's first line (2 positions) is already filled, new invitees are placed deeper into the structure. Participants from upline sponsors may automatically be placed into a user's structure (Spillover). Users receive full marketing rewards and profit from these spillover placements just as they would from their own direct invites. Multi-accounting is strictly prohibited.
