## How does placement work?

<Steps>
  <Step title="First-level positions fill first">
    The first direct registrations fill the available first-level positions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The weakest-branch algorithm places the rest">
    Additional registrations are placed under the branch selected by the active weakest-branch algorithm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spillover may occur">
    Spillover may place a participant inside another user's network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Placement alone does not guarantee a reward">
    Eligibility depends on tier, depth, qualifying activity, active phase, and remaining Protocol Reward Cap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Partner Network Depth

Each NFT tier defines the maximum network depth included in eligible Partner Network Distribution calculations. Current Phase 1 values are shown in the [NFT tier table](/en/nft-ecosystem#all-10-tiers-at-a-glance). Levels 16–22 require the documented governance activation.

## Network Capacity

A complete 22-level binary structure contains **8,388,606 positions** (more than 8.3 million), excluding the root position.
