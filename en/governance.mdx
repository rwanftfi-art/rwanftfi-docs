## Current Allocation

RWANFTFI Corporation holds **30%** of the supply. Twenty Guardian wallets hold **3.5%** each. Under the current allocation, the Corporation can independently meet the proposal threshold; an individual Guardian cannot.

## Future Redistribution

The documented future model targets 30% for the Corporation, 30% across 20 Guardians, and 40% across up to 80 Leader wallets. The new structure becomes operative only after the transfers are completed and publicly verifiable on-chain.

## What can governance change?

- Adjust parameters within their documented on-chain ranges (see [Contract Parameters](/en/contract-parameters)).
- Approve Phase 2 activation.
- Approve documented partner-structure transfers and wallet-change requests.
- Restrict or restore specified platform functionality associated with an NFT.

## Platform Function Restriction

<Warning>
  A successful governance decision may restrict specified platform functions associated with an NFT. The NFT remains in the holder's wallet. A separate governance decision is required to reverse the restriction.
</Warning>
