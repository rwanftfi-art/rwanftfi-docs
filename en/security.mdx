## Phishing Protection

<Warning>
  Use only official RWANFTFI domains and contract addresses. RWANFTFI representatives should never request private keys, seed phrases, or payments to unofficial wallet addresses. Always verify you are on **rwanftfi.com** before connecting your wallet or entering credentials.
</Warning>
