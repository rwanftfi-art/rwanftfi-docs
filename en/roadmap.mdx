The strategic development of RWANFTFI is structured in phases, evolving from a structural marketing launch into a comprehensive global financial ecosystem.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Phase 1: Pre-Launch & Founding (Q2–Q3 2026)">
    **Q2 2026:** Official launch of structural marketing and GIFT NFTs.
    **Q3 2026:** First FinPro (Financial Products) offerings go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 2: Ecosystem Upgrade (Q4 2026)">
    Marketing tree unlocks to full 22 levels. INFINITY NFT (Level 10) launches. VIP Edition and Ambassador NFTs minted (100 units each).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 3: Financial Infrastructure & RWA (2027–2028)">
    **Q1–Q2 2027:** NFTM Mining, DA generation, Lending protocol, and crypto debit card go live.
    **Q3 2027–2028:** RWA platform launches — tokenization of real estate, infrastructure, and business assets. VERA AI personal assistant integrated.
  </Step>
</Steps>
