<Steps>
  <Step title="Q2 2026 · Completed" icon="circle-check">
    - UKIPO filing for the RWANFT word mark (7 April) and the final CertiK audit report (28 April).
    - Platform launch: White List until 15 May, open registration from 18 May.
    - Bug bounty program through CertiK; brandbook at brandbook.rwanftfi.com.
    - First DAO votes; a business stolen from a hacked account returned to its verified owner by DAO vote.
    - Phase 2 activated by DAO vote (15 June), including Infinity (L10) sales; Fast Start Program (18 June).
    - AML Checker and the cross-chain bridge at bridge.rwanftfi.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Q3 2026 · Completed" icon="circle-check">
    - FinPro launched with its first product, FinFlow (8 July); FinPro moved to rwafinpro.com in August.
    - Referral links moved to share.rwanftfi.com (August).
    - RWA NFT Cards: the Visa debit card presented on 20 August, sales open since 29 September.
    - Private Web3 chats on XMTP in the platform messenger.
    - Accumulative Balance lifetime extended to 180 days by DAO vote (September).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Q4 2026 · In progress" icon="circle-half-stroke">
    - FinFlow Promotion 2026, until 31 December.
    - First Purchase NFT and Upgrade NFT promotions, 21 October – 21 November.
    - FinPro Marketing: the Claim Spot pre-launch, then the FinCore launch; dates are announced separately.
    - Debit card top-up with any popular cryptocurrency through the built-in bridge.
    - DAO votes on the Accumulative timer fix and the return to a 120-day lifetime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2027 · Planned" icon="circle">
    - Activation of NFTM Mining, DA Farming, and DA Lending.
    - The first Deflationary Cycle.
    - The start of RWA tokenization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Q3 2027 – 2028 · Long-term" icon="circle">
    - RWA verification framework and the first verified asset integrations.
    - RWA and infrastructure partnerships.
    - The VERA AI assistant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Levels 20–22 of Infinity, the Ambassador and VIP Edition NFTs, and the wider distribution of governance wallets are documented plans without a date; each takes effect only after its DAO vote or launch announcement.

<Note>
  Roadmap dates are targets. Product status, deployed contracts, provider confirmation, and on-chain activation are the source of truth — verify on [BscScan](https://bscscan.com/) and the [CertiK Skynet directory](https://skynet.certik.com/projects/rwanftfi).
</Note>
