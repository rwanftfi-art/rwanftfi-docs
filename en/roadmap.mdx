## Phase 1 — Pre-Launch & Founding (Q2–Q3 2026)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Q2 2026 — Marketing Launch">
    - Structural marketing system goes live with the 22-level binary tree contract (Phase 1: depths 1–15 active, 16–22 reserved for DAO vote).
    - Gift NFTs deployed (even token IDs) for promotional placements.
    - Sponsor Bonus active at 30% on new sales / 20% on rebuys.
    - Auto-Buy 3/3 system enabled for tiers L1–L8.
    - Telegram bot integration for TAN codes and security alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Q3 2026 — Financial Products & FinPro">
    - First FinPro offerings open to early participants.
    - Accumulative Balance lifecycle (120-day window, 70/30 redistribution on expiry) active.
    - Voucher system live (5 USDT minimum, 365-day lifetime).
    - Cross-chain deposit service (`SERVICE_ROLE`) accepting USDT from supported chains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Phase 2 — Ecosystem Upgrade (Q4 2026)

<Steps>
  <Step title="DAO unlock of marketing levels 16–22">
    The DAO votes (30% submission / 50% pass thresholds) to enable Tree Distribution percentages on levels 16–22 (planned: 2% / 2% / 1% / 0.5% / 0.5% / 0.5% / 0.5%). Sponsor Bonus on new sales adjusts to 20%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Elite NFTs launch">
    Infinity (L10, 24,000 USDT, 70,000 USDT Income Limit, 19→22 levels of depth) goes live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limited editions">
    Ambassador NFT (100 units, no income limits, dedicated reward system) and VIP Edition NFT (100 units, gated by Leader Career Rank) released.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Governance redistribution begins">
    Guardians begin gradual redistribution toward up to 80 Leader wallets (0.5% each), expanding governance from 21 to up to 101 wallets without minting new GovTokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Phase 3 — Financial Infrastructure & RWA (2027–2028)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Q1–Q2 2027 — Core Engine">
    - NFTM Mining and DA Farming production-ready for L5+ NFTs.
    - DA Lending (fixed 70% LTV, multi-batch collateral, one-time 5% commission) opens.
    - Crypto debit card program activated; SWAP and card commissions feed the DA Liquidity Pool.
    - First biannual Deflationary Cycle scheduled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Q3 2027 onward — RWA & AI">
    - Real estate tokenization onboarded as RWA collateral; rental and asset income feed the DA Liquidity Pool.
    - Infrastructure assets and commercial RWA partners onboarded.
    - VERA AI assistant integrated into the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Phase milestones depend on DAO governance outcomes (30% / 50% thresholds) and market conditions. Calendar dates are targets; on-chain activations are the source of truth — verify on [BscScan](https://bscscan.com/) and the [CertiK Skynet directory](https://skynet.certik.com/projects/rwanftfi).
