## NFT Purchase Distribution

<Note>
  **Phase 2 values.** The distribution below has applied since Phase 2 was activated by DAO vote in June 2026.
</Note>

| Parameter | First Purchase | Rebuy | Range |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| Sponsor Bonus | 20% | 17% + 1–5% | 10% – 35% |
| Token Reserve (DA Pool) | 15% | 18% | 0% – 30% / 10% – 30% |
| Platform Operations | 2% | 2% | 2% – 10% |
| Accumulative Balance | 20% | 20% | 15% – 30% |
| Tree Distribution Budget | 43% | Residual | Derived |

The rebuy Sponsor Bonus is a 17% base rate plus 1–5% depending on the level at which the personally invited partner is placed. The Token Reserve range is 0% – 30% for a first purchase and 10% – 30% for a rebuy. The Tree Distribution Budget is the residual after the other allocations.

## Timers & Limits

| Parameter | Current | Range |
|---|:---:|---|
| Accumulative Balance lifetime | 180 days (temporary) | 90 – 180 days |
| NFTM claim window | 72 hours | 24 – 96 hours |
| Gift NFTs per address | 100 | 1 – 400 |
| Auto-Sell periods | 120 / 90 / 90 / 65 days | Documented ranges |

**Accumulative Balance lifetime.** Extended from 120 to 180 days by DAO vote in September 2026 as a temporary measure while a fix is prepared: the lifetime timer should reset when the balance reaches zero. A separate DAO vote is planned to restore 120 days after the fix.
