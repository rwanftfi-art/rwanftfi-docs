## What problems do traditional NFTs leave unsolved?

Many NFT products focus primarily on ownership, collectibility, or secondary-market trading. After acquisition, holders may have limited access to continuing product utility or protocol functions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Traditional NFTs" icon="circle-xmark">

  </Card>
  <Card title="RWANFTFI NFTs" icon="circle-check">
    Designed as access credentials for documented protocol utilities and eligibility.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How does RWANFTFI solve this?

RWANFTFI assigns defined access rights and eligibility conditions to each NFT tier. Depending on tier and product status, holders may access partner-program mechanics, DA mining and farming, lending, governance, and future RWA integrations.
