Frozen Balance applies when a direct invitee makes their first NFT purchase or triggers Autobuy and the resulting Sponsor Bonus exceeds the holder's remaining Protocol Reward Cap. The mechanism temporarily holds the excess so the holder can meet the applicable release conditions.

## When does Frozen Balance trigger?

Frozen Balance activates only when all three conditions are met:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The buyer is the holder's direct personal invitee">
    Not a spillover placement or a deeper network participant — specifically a person the holder personally invited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The event is the invitee's first NFT purchase or Autobuy">
    Manual repeat purchases and upgrades by the same invitee are not covered — see the section on repeat purchases below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Sponsor Bonus exceeds the holder's remaining Protocol Reward Cap">
    If the remaining Protocol Reward Cap can absorb the full amount, no freeze occurs — the reward is credited under the standard rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How does the commission amount split?

- The amount within the remaining Protocol Reward Cap is credited under the current **75/20/5** split.
- The excess above the cap is placed in Frozen Balance for **72 hours**.

## What happens during the 72-hour window?

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="You upgrade your NFT">
    If an eligible higher-tier NFT provides sufficient Protocol Reward Cap, the buffered amount is released and credited under the standard split. The released amount reduces the new NFT's remaining cap.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Autobuy is enabled">
    Autobuy may renew the current NFT first and refresh the Protocol Reward Cap. The Sponsor Bonus is then recalculated against the refreshed cap. Only any remaining excess is placed in Frozen Balance.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="You miss the deadline">
    If the release conditions are not met within 72 hours, the amount is redistributed under the current protocol parameters: the configured share is routed to the DA Liquidity Pool, and the remaining share follows the sponsor-route and cascade rules.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Repeat Purchases from the Same Invitee

Frozen Balance covers the qualifying first purchase and Autobuy events. Manual repeat purchases and upgrades by the same invitee follow the standard partner-reward, missed-reward, and compression rules.

## High-Volume Scenario — Routing Through the Structure

For network-depth activity, the Protocol Reward Cap and Autobuy rules are applied first. Any remaining amount that cannot be credited is routed according to the active compression rules. Frozen Balance does not activate when the source is Partner Network Distribution rather than a qualifying direct first purchase or Autobuy.

## Frozen Balance Parameters (On-Chain)

| Parameter | Value | Mutability |
|---|---|---|
| Freeze window | 72 hours | Immutable |
| DA Liquidity Pool share after expiry | 70% | DAO-adjustable within documented range |
| Sponsor-route share after expiry | 30% | DAO-adjustable within documented range |
| Trigger | First purchase or Autobuy by direct invitee | Immutable |
| Release distribution | 75/20/5 | Standard split — see [Architecture](/en/architecture) |
