Marketing rewards in RWANFTFI consist of three independent streams that combine to form your total income:

1. **Sponsor Bonus** — Direct commission for inviting a partner (30% Phase 1 / 20% Phase 2 of NFT sale price).
2. **Tree Distribution** — Per-level rewards from your downline structure (levels 2–22, percentages vary by depth).
3. **Matching Bonus** — Secondary 5% + 5% + 5% from your direct partners' income across 3 levels.

Each stream is calculated and paid independently by the smart contract on every qualifying NFT purchase.

## Tree Distribution

Earn a percentage from every NFT sale in your downline, across up to 22 levels deep:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Levels 1–6 · Entry Layers" icon="layer-group">
    - Level 1: 0%
    - Level 2: 1%
    - Levels 3–4: 2% each
    - Levels 5–6: 3% each
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Levels 7–15 · Core Earning Depth" icon="chart-simple">
    - Levels 7–14: 4% each (8 levels × 4% = main income driver)
    - Level 15: 5% (highest single-level percentage)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Levels 16–22 · Phase 2 Expansion" icon="unlock">
    - Levels 16–17: 2% each
    - Level 18: 1%
    - Levels 19–22: 0.5% each

    *Unlocked in Phase 2 via DAO governance vote (planned for Q4 2026). Requires INFINITY NFT for full depth.*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  **Why is Level 1 = 0%?** Tree Distribution and Sponsor Bonus are two separate reward streams. Your direct invites sit on Level 1 of your binary tree, and you already earn a Sponsor Bonus (30% in Phase 1, 20% in Phase 2) from their NFT purchase. Level 1 of Tree Distribution is set to 0% to prevent double-compensation. Tree Distribution rewards begin from Level 2 (1%) onward — covering activity deeper in your structure beyond your personal invites.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Spillover on Level 1 earns nothing directly.** When a partner is placed on your Level 1 via spillover (your upline's invite lands in your tree), you earn 0% Sponsor Bonus (it goes to their actual inviter) and 0% Tree Distribution (Level 1 = 0%). The value of spillover is realized from Level 2 and deeper — when those spillover partners build their own teams, their recruits generate tree distribution rewards for you.
</Note>

## Compression

<Tip>
  **Compression works in your favor.** If someone above you has an exhausted income limit or insufficient NFT level, their rewards compress upward — potentially to you. The deeper your NFT's marketing access, the more compressed rewards you can capture.
</Tip>

When a user in your downline is skipped (Income Limit = 0 or NFT level too low), the reward passes upward to the next qualified participant. This is flagged as **Lost Income** for the skipped user.


## Matching Bonus (5% + 5% + 5%)

Earn a percentage of the **NFT-sales income** generated by your direct partners across 3 levels. The Matching Bonus is calculated only from marketing payouts your direct partner receives from NFT sales within their structure — not from other income streams (Frozen Balance releases, manual DA sales, etc.):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Level 1 — 5%">
    Requires at least a Level 4 (ECLIPSE, 275 USDT) NFT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Level 2 — 5%">
    Requires cumulative personal sales of 1,000 USDT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Level 3 — 5%">
    Requires cumulative personal sales of 3,000 USDT.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **What "cumulative personal sales" means:** This is the running lifetime total of all NFT purchases made by your direct invites. Repeat purchases (rebuys, upgrades) by the same partner count toward the total, and sales to different partners accumulate together. For example, if one partner buys three NFTs at 1,000 USDT each, your cumulative total is 3,000 USDT — unlocking Matching Bonus Level 3. Once a threshold is reached, that Matching Bonus level is **permanently active** and does not reset.
</Info>
