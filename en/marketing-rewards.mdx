Protocol rewards are distributions calculated by the smart contract from documented allocations of qualifying NFT purchases. They are not interest, yield, or a return on investment. Every reward requires a qualifying purchase event, an eligible position, and remaining Protocol Reward Cap — and the total that can be distributed for any purchase is bounded by the contract's allocation budget.

<Note>
  **How rewards are funded and bounded:**
  - **Funded per event.** Each reward is calculated from a specific qualifying NFT purchase under the current contract parameters — never from new registrations or placement by themselves.
  - **Bounded by budget.** The network-distribution budget is a calculated residual of the purchase allocation (see [Contract Parameters](/en/contract-parameters)); the protocol never distributes more than the documented allocation.
  - **Bounded by caps.** Credited rewards consume the recipient's Protocol Reward Cap. Amounts that cannot be credited follow the compression and residual-budget rules.
</Note>

## Sponsor Bonus

A reward calculated from a qualifying direct NFT purchase, at the current Phase 1 rates: **30%** for a first qualifying purchase and **20%** for a rebuy. Any Phase 2 change requires the documented governance activation.

## Partner Network Distribution (Tree Distribution)

A per-level reward calculation within the recipient's eligible network depth:

| Network Level | Current Rate |
|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | 0% |
| 2 | 1% |
| 3–4 | 2% each |
| 5–6 | 3% each |
| 7–14 | 4% each |
| 15 | 5% |
| 16–22 | 0% until governance activation |

## Matching Bonus

A conditional reward across up to three qualifying levels:

| Matching Level | Rate | Eligibility |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | 5% | Eligible NFT tier |
| 2 | 5% | 1,000 USDT cumulative volume of qualifying direct purchases |
| 3 | 5% | 3,000 USDT cumulative volume of qualifying direct purchases |

## Standard Reward Split

Eligible protocol rewards are currently split under the documented **75/20/5** allocation between Regular Balance, Accumulative Balance, and the DA Liquidity Pool.

## Compression

If a network position does not meet the applicable eligibility conditions, the amount is routed according to the active compression and residual-budget rules. The exact routing matches the current contract parameters — see [Contract Parameters](/en/contract-parameters).
