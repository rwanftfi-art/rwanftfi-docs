The partner program uses three reward types: **Sponsor Bonus**, **Tree Distribution**, and **Matching Bonus**. Each reward depends on NFT tier, network relationship, qualifying activity, protocol phase, and remaining Protocol Reward Cap.

## Sponsor Bonus

The Sponsor Bonus is calculated from a qualifying direct NFT purchase. The current Phase 1 rates are **30%** for a new sale and **20%** for a rebuy. Any Phase 2 change requires the documented governance activation.

## Tree Distribution

| Network Level | Current Rate |
|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | 0% |
| 2 | 1% |
| 3–4 | 2% each |
| 5–6 | 3% each |
| 7–14 | 4% each |
| 15 | 5% |
| 16–22 | 0% until governance activation |

## Matching Bonus

| Matching Level | Rate | Eligibility |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | 5% | Eligible NFT tier |
| 2 | 5% | 1,000 USDT cumulative personal sales |
| 3 | 5% | 3,000 USDT cumulative personal sales |

## Standard Reward Split

Eligible partner rewards are currently split under the documented **75/20/5** allocation between Regular Balance, Accumulative Balance, and the DA Liquidity Pool.

## Compression

If a network position does not meet the applicable eligibility conditions, the amount is routed according to the active compression and residual-budget rules. The exact routing matches the current contract parameters — see [Contract Parameters](/en/contract-parameters).
