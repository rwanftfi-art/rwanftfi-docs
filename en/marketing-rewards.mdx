The Sponsor Bonus is calculated from a qualifying direct NFT purchase. The current rates have applied since Phase 2 was activated by DAO vote in June 2026.

## Standard Reward Split

Eligible partner rewards are currently split under the documented **75/20/5** allocation between Regular Balance, Accumulative Balance, and the DA Liquidity Pool.

- **Gift NFTs.** Rewards on a Gift NFT are split 50/50 between the Regular Balance and the Accumulative Balance.
- **FinPro Marketing.** The separate FinPro Marketing program credits 100% of its rewards to the marketing balance, without the 75/20/5 split and without the 5% fee. See [FinPro Marketing](/en/finpro-marketing).

## Compression

If a network position does not meet the applicable eligibility conditions, the amount is routed according to the active compression and residual-budget rules. The exact routing matches the current contract parameters — see [Contract Parameters](/en/contract-parameters).
