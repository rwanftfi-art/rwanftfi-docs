# Deflationary Asset (DA)

The core economic engine of the RWANFTFI ecosystem is the Deflationary Asset (DA). This token model is engineered to be 100% backed by USDT, with deflationary mechanics where tokens are burned upon sale, mathematically reducing supply relative to the liquidity pool.

## Token Mechanics & Price Model

The DA token operates on the Binance Smart Chain (BEP20) as an upgradeable ERC20 token with 6 decimals. 

**Core Parameters:**
* **Maximum Supply:** Hard-capped at 21,000,000 (twenty-one million) tokens.
* **Initial Price:** 1 USDT per DA.
* **Transferability:** Peer-to-peer transfers of DA tokens between users are strictly forbidden at the smart contract level. The token can only be interacted with via official ecosystem functions (sell, loan, repay).
* **Liquidity Pool:** Every income source in the ecosystem feeds into the DA liquidity pool. A 5% commission-tax on all marketing rewards is directly routed to this pool. This 5% commission is deducted from marketing rewards at the time of accrual. It is separate from and unrelated to the sell percentages (75% manual / 70% auto-sell) described in [Selling & Lending](/deflationary-asset#selling-lending).

**The "Only Upward" Price Formula:**
The price of DA is determined dynamically by the smart contract based on the ratio of the USDT liquidity pool to the total circulating supply of DA.

`Price = Liquidity ÷ Circulating Supply`

*(The smart contract internally uses 6-decimal precision: `Price = (Liquidity × 10^6) / TotalSupply`, but the economic principle is straightforward — price equals liquidity divided by supply.)*

Because tokens are burned upon sale (reducing supply) while fees remain in the pool (maintaining or increasing liquidity), the mathematical output of this formula constantly drives the price upward.

## Mining & Farming Pipeline

To acquire DA, users must participate in a two-step process: Mining and Farming. Only Premium (Level 5+) and Elite NFTs are capable of this.

**Step 1: Mining (Generating NFTM)**
When a user activates mining on their eligible NFT, it begins generating NFTM over a specific cycle duration based on the NFT level (ranging from 45 days for Level 5 down to 40 days for Level 10). 

Once the mining cycle completes and the NFTM mining counter is credited to the user's account, the user has exactly **72 hours** to claim it and place it into Staking/Farming. If the user fails to stake the NFTM within this 72-hour window, the cycle is lost, and they must restart the mining process from scratch.

The amount of NFTM generated per mining cycle is determined by the NFT level:
* **Levels 5–7:** Cycle 1: 10% of NFT price; Cycle 2: 10% of NFT price.
* **Levels 8–10:** Cycle 1: 10% of NFT price; Cycle 2: **15%** of NFT price (enhanced generation for top-tier levels).

**Step 2: Farming (Converting to DA)**
Once staked, the NFTM enters the Farming phase. The duration of the farming cycle mirrors the mining cycle (40–45 days). At the end of this cycle, the NFTM mining counter is converted into actual DA based on the current DA price, and the DA is credited to the user's TokenStack (a smart contract structure that tracks DA holdings and progressive unlock periods).

## Selling & Lending

Once a user holds DA, they have two primary financial options: selling the asset or using it as collateral for a loan.

**Selling DA & Burn Mechanics:**
When selling DA, there are two distinct scenarios with different conditions:

1. **Manual Sale (user sells independently):**
   * The user receives **75%** of the value in USDT.
   * **25%** of the tokens are permanently burned.

2. **Auto-Sell (triggered after TokenStack period expires):**
   * The user receives **70%** of the value in USDT.
   * **30%** of the tokens are permanently burned.
   * Triggers automatically if the user has not sold manually before the period expires.

In both cases, the sold tokens are permanently removed from circulation, which mathematically increases the price of DA for the remaining holders. Auto-sell offers a 5% lower payout, incentivizing users to actively manage their DA positions.

**Auto-Sell Periods:** To prevent massive simultaneous sell-offs, DA is subject to progressive unlocking periods within a user's TokenStack:
* Period 1: 25% available to sell
* Period 2: 40% available to sell
* Period 3: 50% available to sell
* Period 4: 100% available to sell

**Lending (Credit against DA):**
Instead of selling and losing their position, users can take a loan in USDT against their DA holdings. Users can borrow up to **70%** of the current USDT value of their DA collateral (Loan-to-Value). The smart contract enforces a strict 30-day loan cutoff period. If a loan is not managed within the required parameters, the collateralized TokenStack can be liquidated.
