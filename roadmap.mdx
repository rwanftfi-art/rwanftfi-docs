# Roadmap

The strategic development of RWANFTFI is structured in phases, evolving from a structural marketing launch into a comprehensive global financial ecosystem.

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch & Founding (Q2–Q3 2026)
* **Q2 2026 (Pre-Launch):** Official launch of the structural marketing system and introduction of GIFT NFTs to begin building the initial user base.
* **Q3 2026 (Founding Phase I):** Rollout of the first FinPro (Financial Products) offerings, providing early ecosystem participants with new earning opportunities.

## Phase 2: Ecosystem Upgrade (Q4 2026)
* **Marketing Expansion:** The structural marketing tree fully unlocks from 15 levels down to the maximum 22 levels.
* **Elite NFTs:** Official launch of the INFINITY NFT (Level 10).
* **Exclusive Assets:** Minting and distribution of the highly limited VIP Edition NFTs (100 units) and Ambassador NFTs (100 units).

## Phase 3: Financial Infrastructure & RWA (2027–2028)
* **Q1–Q2 2027 (Official Launch Financial Infrastructure):** The core economic engine goes live. This includes the activation of NFTM Mining, the generation of DA (Deflationary Assets), the rollout of the Lending protocol (borrowing against DA), and the introduction of a crypto debit card.
* **Q3 2027 – 2028 (RWA & Global Expansion):** Launch of the dedicated RWA platform. This phase focuses on the tokenization of real estate, infrastructure, and business assets, fully integrating the real economy with the RWANFTFI digital infrastructure. The platform will also feature the integration of an AI personal assistant, VERA AI.
