# Marketing Structure & Rewards

The RWANFTFI platform utilizes a sophisticated, smart contract-driven structural marketing system. It is designed to reward users for expanding the network, with mechanics that ensure infinite compression and mathematical sustainability.

## Binary Tree & Placement Algorithm

When a user activates an NFT (either Regular or Gift), they are permanently placed into a binary marketing structure. The structure has a maximum depth of 22 levels. When fully filled to Level 22, the tree holds exactly **8,388,606 participant positions**, growing exponentially at each level (2 on Level 1, 4 on Level 2, 8 on Level 3, and so on).

The smart contract automatically places new users using a "weakest branch, left to right" algorithm. A new participant is permanently linked to their sponsor. If a sponsor's first line (2 positions) is already filled, new invitees are placed deeper into the structure. Participants from upline sponsors may automatically be placed into a user's structure (Spillover). Users receive full marketing rewards and profit from these spillover placements just as they would from their own direct invites. Multi-accounting is strictly prohibited.

## Reward Distribution

Rewards are generated whenever an NFT is sold or repurchased within a user's downline. The depth from which a user can earn depends entirely on their NFT level.

**Sponsor Bonus (Personal Sale Rewards):**
When a user directly sells an NFT to a new participant, the sponsor earns a percentage of the sale price. This percentage differs between the two phases of the project:

* **Phase 1:** Personal NFT sale yields **30%** to the sponsor. Repeat NFT purchase (rebuy) yields **20%**.
* **Phase 2:** Personal NFT sale adjusts to **20%**. Repeat NFT purchase remains **20%**.

These percentages are separate from the tree distribution rewards described below. (Note: Levels 16–22 will be fully unlocked in Phase 2 of the project).

**Tree Distribution — Level Distribution Percentages (Out of 22 Levels):**
* Level 1: 0%
* Level 2: 1%
* Levels 3-4: 2% each
* Levels 5-6: 3% each
* Levels 7-14: 4% each
* Level 15: 5%
* Levels 16-17: 2% each
* Level 18: 1%
* Levels 19-22: 0.5% each

**Compression:** The system features an automatic "infinite compression" algorithm. Compression is a smart contract mechanism that skips users whose Income Limit is zero or who do not have the required NFT level to earn from a specific depth — the reward passes upward to the next eligible user. If an NFT sale occurs in a user's downline but the user's NFT level does not grant access to that depth, the reward bypasses them (this is flagged as Lost Income). Similarly, if a user's Income Limit reaches zero, they are temporarily skipped. In both cases, the reward compresses upward to the next qualified, active participant.

## Matching Bonus

The Matching Bonus allows users to earn a percentage of the income generated by their direct partners across 3 levels of depth. The bonus is structured as **5% + 5% + 5%**.

**Unlock Requirements:**
* **Level 1 (5%):** Requires the user to own at least a Level 4 (ECLIPSE, 275 USDT) NFT.
* **Level 2 (5%):** Requires the user to achieve cumulative personal sales of 1,000 USDT.
* **Level 3 (5%):** Requires the user to achieve cumulative personal sales of 3,000 USDT.
